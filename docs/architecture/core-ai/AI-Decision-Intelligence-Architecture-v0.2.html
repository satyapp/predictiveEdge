AI Decision Intelligence Architecture

One AI recommendation authority, twelve mandatory intelligence resources, positive-edge readiness gates, continuous Trade Guardian reassessment and strict binary accountability.

Authority boundary

The application creates governed facts, deterministic features, probability evidence, feasibility estimates and immutable records. AI Decision Intelligence alone creates recommendations. Readiness and safety gates can reject or force abstention; they never invent or reverse a recommendation.

Non-negotiable: Risk, Portfolio, Learning, Point-in-Time Quality, Regime/Drift, Execution, Validation and Calibration are mandatory. Missing, stale, cross-user, unvalidated or negative-edge context cannot produce an actionable recommendation.
1

Application builds truth

Causal facts, deterministic features, execution estimates, validation results, calibrated probabilities and evidence manifests.

2

AI owns recommendation

AI Decision Intelligence is the sole origin of BUY, SELL, WAIT, NO_TRADE and linked management advice.

3

Trader remains in control

The recommendation is advisory. The trader accepts, rejects, watches and executes manually.

Promotion evidence

  • Chronological walk-forward and untouched holdout
  • Costs, spread, slippage, latency and impact
  • Baseline and regime comparison
  • Shadow/paper observation
  • Approval, expiry and rollback policy

Success metrics

  • Strict directional WIN/LOSS
  • Net expectancy and profit factor
  • Maximum drawdown and tail losses
  • Probability calibration and coverage
  • Regime and confidence-bucket stability

Strict outcome accountability

Every BUY/SELL remains a binary model outcome, but promotion decisions also measure whether the strategy produces positive expectancy without unacceptable drawdown.
